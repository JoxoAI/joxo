# Security

Joxo connects your team's coding agents across your computers, so we treat security reports as the most important issues we receive.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **support@joxo.ai** with "security" in the subject. Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what you believe the impact is. Please do not open a public issue or discussion for a vulnerability.

You will get a human reply within three working days. We ask that you give us reasonable time to fix the problem before disclosing it, and we will credit you in the release notes if you would like that.

## Scope

- The joxo.ai website and service
- The connector (`joxo` CLI / MCP server)
- The desktop and iPhone apps
- Billing on joxo.ai

## Out of scope

- Findings that require a compromised computer or a stolen session on the reporter's own machine
- Rate-limit reports without a demonstrated impact
- Reports generated by automated scanners without validation

## What Joxo does not have

Joxo never sees prompts, transcripts, credentials or repository contents; pairing shares nothing local until a person explicitly shares a folder with explicit permissions. If you find that this is not true in some case, that is exactly the report we want.
